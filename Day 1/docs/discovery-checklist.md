# Codes Training Center — Discovery Checklist

Write the client’s answer beside every question. Do not design the solution until unclear answers have been discussed.

## Business

- Why does Codes Training Center want to replace manual registration now?
- Which current step costs the most time or creates the most errors?
- What business result should improve in the first month?
- Who approves the final scope and accepts delivery?

## Users

- What can a visitor see without an account?
- What information must a student provide?
- Which employee creates courses and decides registration status?
- Does a manager need edit access or only summaries?
- Are instructors part of this MVP?

## Workflow

- How does a visitor become a registered student?
- Is every valid registration automatically confirmed or reviewed by staff?
- What happens when a course is full?
- Can a student cancel, or must staff cancel?
- What happens to registrations when a course is archived?

## Data

- Which course fields are required in English and Arabic?
- Which student fields are necessary for the MVP?
- How long should enrollment records be retained?
- Which statuses are enough for the first release?
- Which staff roles may view student email addresses?

## Constraints

- What is the delivery date and review schedule?
- Which devices/browsers matter most?
- What hosting budget and domain already exist?
- Which integrations are explicitly excluded?

## Success and acceptance

- Can a visitor find and register for a published course in under two minutes?
- Can an admin create, edit, publish, and archive a bilingual course?
- Can an admin review registration status without a spreadsheet?
- Does the workflow remain usable on a 375px mobile screen?
- Can the client explain what is included and excluded from the MVP?
