Contrast contract

Use Ink on Cream/White, Cream on Ink, and White on Coral/Danger. Mint is a focus/support color and should not carry small white text. Semantic meaning must always include text or an icon—not color alone.

07 · Responsive & accessibility

The same content contract works at every width.

Breakpoint rules

Base
One column, full-width actions, horizontal mobile nav.
sm · 640
Two-column facts and short field groups.
md · 768
Desktop nav appears; data tables may scroll.
lg · 1024
Main content and decision sidebar can sit together.
xl · 1280
Admin form and course list share a wide workspace.

Accessibility contract

  • One descriptive H1 and logical heading order.
  • Visible labels, hints, and connected error messages.
  • Keyboard access and visible Mint focus indicators.
  • Links navigate; buttons perform actions.
  • Status uses text or icons in addition to color.
  • Touch targets are approximately 44×44 CSS pixels.
  • Motion respects reduced-motion preferences.
  • Loading, empty, success, and error states are announced.
