# Codes Training Center Help Center

This guide explains how to use the system. Use the language button in the header to switch every visible label between English and Arabic; Arabic mode also changes the page direction.

## Browse courses

1. Choose **Courses / الدورات** in the header.
2. Review the course level, duration, and short description.
3. Choose **View course / عرض الدورة** for full details.

Draft and archived courses are intentionally hidden from visitors.

## Create a student account

1. Choose **Join now / انضم الآن**.
2. Enter your full name, a valid email, and a password of at least 8 characters.
3. Choose **Create account / إنشاء حساب**.

The system signs you in and returns you to the course catalog. If the email is already registered, use the login page instead.

## Register for a course

1. Sign in with a student account.
2. Open a published course.
3. Choose **Register for this course / التسجيل في الدورة**.
4. Wait for the success message.
5. Open **My registrations / تسجيلاتي** to track the result.

Each student can register for a course once. Registration can also stop when the course reaches capacity.

## Understand registration status

- **Pending / قيد المراجعة:** staff has not made a decision yet.
- **Confirmed / مؤكد:** registration is accepted.
- **Cancelled / ملغي:** registration will not continue.

## Staff course workflow (Admin)

1. Sign in with an admin account and open **Dashboard**.
2. Choose **Manage courses**.
3. Add both English and Arabic titles/summaries, duration, capacity, and level.
4. Select **Publish now** only when the public content is ready.
5. Use **Edit** to correct/update a course.
6. Use **Archive** to remove it from public browsing without deleting enrollment history.

## Staff enrollment workflow

1. From Dashboard choose **Review enrollments**.
2. Find the student and course.
3. Admins choose pending, confirmed, or cancelled and save the status.
4. Managers can review the table and dashboard totals but cannot change records.

## Common questions

**Why can’t I see a course?** It may still be a draft or may have been archived.

**Why can’t I register?** Confirm you are signed in as a student, have not already registered, and the course is not full.

**Why can’t a manager edit?** The manager role is intentionally read-only. Course and status changes require an admin.

**How do I sign out?** Choose **Log out / تسجيل الخروج** in the header, especially on shared computers.
